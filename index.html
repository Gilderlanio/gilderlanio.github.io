Laboratório de Bioinformática e Ciências de Dados (LBCD)

Laboratory of Bioinformatics and Data Science · Institute of Biological Sciences (ICB) · Federal University of Pará (UFPA)

The Bioinformatics and Data Science Laboratory provides a dedicated environment to support scientific research by integrating high-performance computing infrastructure, large-scale data storage and processing resources, and specialized bioinformatics pipelines. Its activities encompass product development as well as the implementation and maintenance of software, web platforms, APIs, and scientific databases aimed at organizing, integrating, and exploring biological information.
By combining expertise in bioinformatics, data science, molecular biology, and computer science, the laboratory seeks to advance investigations into complex biological systems, uncover relevant patterns and associations, and generate knowledge from "omics" data. Our work contributes to strengthening interdisciplinary research, developing technological innovation solutions, advancing science, and training human resources.
